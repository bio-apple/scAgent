# Wherry 2011 — T cell exhaustion

Nat Rev Immunol. Canonical review of exhausted T cells (Tex).

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/nri3156
- PMID: 21739672

## Constraints on annotation claims

Labeling a Leiden cluster as “exhausted T / CD8 Tex” requires all of:

1. Checkpoint high expression: PDCD1 (PD-1), HAVCR2 (TIM-3), usually LAG3 / TOX. PDCD1 alone is insufficient.
2. Pathway term + statistic: e.g. GO:0002429 p/FDR—not “looks exhausted”.
3. Citation: this DOI/PMID, or TIM-3 evidence Sakuishi 2010 JEM https://doi.org/10.1084/jem.20100643 (PMID 20837752).

Supportive evidence chain, not causal proof from intervention. UMAP position or CellTypist labels cannot replace the three legs above.
